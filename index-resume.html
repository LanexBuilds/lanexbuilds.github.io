Research-to-video production pipeline · Self-directed

A single topic form becomes a researched, scripted, voiced, and rendered video through seven automated stages.

  • Built with n8n, Supabase, Google Gemini, and NVIDIA Nemotron across seven pipeline stages.
  • Added a critic stage after each major step that approves, revises, or patches specific lines before the next stage runs.
  • Every run is logged and traceable; a kill switch stops the pipeline cleanly without leaving jobs in limbo.
2026 Live

Local lead-generation and outreach engine · Self-directed

Finds, analyses, scores, and drafts outreach for local businesses — at zero running cost, with a person approving every send.

  • Runs entirely on local open-source models (Ollama) so cost does not scale with volume.
  • Filtering and deduplication stages remove noise before any AI time is spent on it.
  • Drafts are never sent automatically. A person always decides; nothing leaves the system without approval.
2026 In progress

Auto-publishing portfolio engine · Self-directed

The system behind this website. Posts are drafted from a short Slack submission, reviewed, and published on a 48-hour schedule with a person always able to intervene.

  • Supabase is the single source of truth; Notion mirrors published content on its own clock.
  • The 48-hour auto-publish timer lives in the database, so publishing works even when the automation layer is offline.
  • Every draft carries a Slack approval card with approve, reject, and hold actions.

About

I build systems that keep people on the decisions that matter.

Mechanical engineering student, workflow automation builder, and — for the last two years — someone who can't stop noticing processes that could run themselves.

I'm Lan, a mechanical engineering student at the Technological Institute of the Philippines. Most of what I do outside class is building automated workflows — systems that handle the repetitive parts of a process so people can focus on the parts that actually need judgement.

I got into this backwards. Most people start with a tool and look for something to use it on. I started with a problem — a lead-generation routine that was eating hours every week — and worked backwards to whatever would fix it. That's still how I work. The tool is the last decision, not the first.

The idea I keep coming back to is this: automation isn't about removing people, it's about putting them in the right places. Every system I've built still has a person in it. But that person is only on the decisions that matter — which leads to send, which video passes quality, which article goes live. Everything else runs on its own.

Right now I'm building the third of three projects: a workflow that keeps this portfolio growing without manual updates. That's what you're reading.
